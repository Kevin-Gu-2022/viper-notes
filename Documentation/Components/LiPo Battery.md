# Parameters
## Capacity
- The capacity of a LiPo battery, measured in mAh (milliampere-hour), indicates how much current you can draw from the battery continuously for an hour until it’s empty
- 5 inch: 900mah - 1300mah

## C Rating
- C Rating is an indicator of the maximum current you can safely draw from a LiPo battery without causing damage. In theory, this can be calculated by:

```
Maximum Current Draw = Capacity x C-Rating
```
- Batteries with higher C-ratings tend to be heavier and larger even at the same capacity

## Connector
- The most common discharge connector is the XT60, mainly used in 5″ FPV drones or larger.
