## Terms
- Just a P controller will have steady state error
- I term eliminates steady state error
- D term predicts future value and helps to prevent overshoot. Increases system responsivity
- REMEMBER: All these are with respect to the **ERROR**!

## Tuning
- High p value increases responsiveness, but too high will cause overcorrection and high frequency oscillations
- High I value stops unwanted drift, but too high will make it unresponsive
- D reduces oscillations caused by O. Setting D too high causes motor vibrations
