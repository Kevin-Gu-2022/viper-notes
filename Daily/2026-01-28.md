## 📌 Notes
- Leading edge always corresponds with direction of rotation (think of how just one side of the blade will scoop up air)
- Joystick is just a P controller, so never actually reaches set-point - steady state error generally always present
- ACRO mode means no attitude adjustment, e.g. controller sends 20$\degree$ pitch *rate* command, which turns the quadcopter by 20$\degree$, but even when joystick goes back to centre, quadcopter still at 20$\degree$ tilt
- STAB mode means that joystick commands correspond to angle tilts
