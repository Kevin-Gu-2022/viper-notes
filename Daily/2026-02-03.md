## 📌 Notes
### Gazebo
- Finally got Gazebo working! Just follow the pictures in the `cr` calibrate RC command to setup controller. The reason thrust control needs to be held down while calibrating pitch and roll is due to quirks in how Flix does the calibration
- Use follow mode and Ctrl R to reset world, teleporting models to initial positions with zero velocity
- Due to weight of drone in simulation, after a little thrust, it eventually falls back down, which aligns with what happens in real life. 50% thrust not enough to sustain hover. 
- Attitude hold clearly working, but drift still present (motors are intentionally not balanced in `gazebo/simulator.cpp`)
- An altitude sensor ToF (Time of Flight) would help to resolve position drift
