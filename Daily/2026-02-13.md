## Existing INDI Controllers
- Potential INDI controller using PX4 but super old: https://discuss.px4.io/t/indi-controller-on-px4/36293
	- Based on this: https://github.com/EwoudSmeur/paparazzi/tree/bebop_indi_experiment
	- Accompanying paper: https://pure.tudelft.nl/ws/files/9188985/Adaptive_Incremental_Nonlinear_Dynamic_Inversion_or_Attitude_Control_of_Micro_Aerial_Vehiclesa.pdf
- PX4 has Cyphal support, and is apparently quite modular, allowing for easy swapping of internal control algorithms
- ArduPilot only has experimental support of Cyphal, and is monolithic, not ideal for this project
