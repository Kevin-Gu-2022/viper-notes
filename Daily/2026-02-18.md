## Powering Pika Spark
- Power from battery comes out of a XT60 connector typically
- Can connect this directly with the power splitting board
- Then, we can pass this through a DC/DC converter to ensure a more or less stable voltage at the input to the PoDL of the Pika Spark
- Will need a connector on end of output cable of DC/DC converter to plug into the JST SM02B-GHS-TB of the Pika Spark
- Because the Ethernet twisted pair is differential, the ground would be 8V above everything else, but it should be fine as everything is galvanically isolated
